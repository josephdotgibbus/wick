Wick

Heat is your life. It burns down on its own, and touching an enemy spends more. Sparks on the ground fill it back up. Shots fire at the nearest enemy. Clear every enemy in a room to open its doors. Each dungeon has one treasure room with three pedestals. A passive changes your stats. An active fills the one slot, and a later one can replace it. Take only one pedestal. The last room is the Briar.

The same seed repeats the rooms, the coins, and where enemies stand. How you move can still differ.
