Wick

Heat is your life. It drains by itself, and touching an enemy spends more. Sparks on the ground fill it back up. Shots fire at the nearest enemy. Clear every enemy to end the round, then pick one upgrade. Every 5th round you fight the Briar and must take a curse.

The same seed repeats the map and the enemy spawns. How you move, and which upgrades you pick, can still differ.
